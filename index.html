In multiplayer, stop by an exclamation-mark NPC and press I (or TALK on touch) for three choices and the chance of a useful buff; everyone else keeps racing. Ignore them to save time. Every run has a seed: type it on the menu to replay the same room, traps, pickups and spinners. One run in twenty is Home Affairs From Hell. Rage fills as you suffer. At 100% press Shift (or X): you are untouchable and fast for four seconds and you can smash the Window 7 gate. During a spinner, 60% rage buys a shout that deletes the KAK slice. Four endings to find.
